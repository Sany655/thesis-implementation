# Expanded Dataset Registry

This registry tracks the new open-access Dengue fever datasets sourced from Mendeley Data, intended for future model expansion, validation, or meta-analysis.

## 1. Dengue Fever Hematological Dataset
* **Authors / Reference:** Nirob et al., 2025
* **Description:** Clinical Insights for Improved Diagnosis and Patient Management.
* **Format & Size:** CSV format containing 1,523 individual patient records and 19 columns.
* **Direct Mendeley Link:** [https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/6fsrsk3mb8](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/6fsrsk3mb8)
* **DOI:** `10.17632/6fsrsk3mb8.2`
* **License:** CC BY 4.0

---

## 2. Predictive Clinical Dataset for Dengue Fever
* **Authors / Reference:** Assaduzzaman / Mim et al., 2024
* **Description:** Predictive Clinical Dataset using Vital Signs and Blood Parameters.
* **Format & Size:** CSV format containing 1,003 individual patient records and 9 clinical parameters.
* **Direct Mendeley Link:** [https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/xrsbyjs24t/1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/xrsbyjs24t/1)
* **DOI:** `10.17632/xrsbyjs24t.1`
* **License:** CC BY 4.0

---

## 3. Integrated Dengue Prediction Dataset
* **Authors / Reference:** Siddiky et al., 2026
* **Description:** Integrated dataset with Clinical Biomarkers, Weather Variables, and Risk Indicators.
* **Format & Size:** XLSX format containing over 1,500 records and 19 columns.
* **Direct Mendeley Link:** [https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/ywp39jnzk3](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/ywp39jnzk3)
* **DOI:** `10.17632/ywp39jnzk3.2`
* **License:** CC BY 4.0
